About the Game

Star Voyager is a thrilling space exploration and combat game that puts you in command of your own starship. Journey through unknown galaxies, engage in epic battles, and build your interstellar empire. Stunning graphics, dynamic gameplay, and a vast universe await!

System Requirements

  • OS: Windows 10 / macOS 10.15 or later
  • Processor: Intel Core i5 or equivalent
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GTX 970 or AMD equivalent
  • Storage: 10 GB available space
